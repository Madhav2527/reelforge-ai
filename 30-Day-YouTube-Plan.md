# 30-Day YouTube Content Calendar: AI & Creator Growth

This 30-day content plan leverages the current massive trend of AI integration in the Creator Economy. It is designed to drive traffic to your app, ReelForge AI, by establishing authority, providing extreme value, and utilizing the high engagement rates of YouTube Shorts combined with the deep connection of Long-form content.

*Focus: Grabbing attention and highlighting creator burnout vs. AI efficiency.*

* **Day 1 (Short):** **"3 AI tools that feel illegal to know (for creators)."** Fast-paced listicle. #3 is ReelForge AI. 
* **Day 2 (Community Post):** Image poll: "How many hours a week do you spend editing?"
* **Day 3 (Long-Form - 8 mins):** **"I Automated My Entire YouTube Workflow (And Got My Life Back)."** A deep dive into using AI to script, generate, and post content. Showcase ReelForge as the central scripting engine.
* **Day 4 (Short):** **"The 5-second hook formula that MrBeast uses."** Educational, text-on-screen, fast edits. 
* **Day 5 (Short):** **"Stop writing your own scripts."** A controversial take on why human scripting is slowing creators down, demonstrating an AI generation in real-time.
* **Day 6 (Community Post):** Behind the scenes screenshot of your ReelForge dashboard.
* **Day 7 (Rest/Analytics):** Review week 1 metrics.
